# Getting Started

Welcome to the POS system. This guide helps with initial setup.

## First Steps
1. **Login**: Default is username `admin`, password `admin`. Change it immediately from Settings.
2. **Configure your store**: Go to *Settings* and enter store name, address, phone, currency, and default tax rate.
3. **Create categories**: From *Products*, add categories to organize your catalog.
4. **Add products**: For each product, enter SKU, barcode, price, cost, tax rate, and initial stock.
5. **Open the drawer**: Before the sales day, open the cash drawer from *Drawer* with your opening amount.
6. **Start selling**: Go to *POS* and start ringing up invoices.

## Roles
- **Admin**: Full access.
- **Manager**: Sales, products, reports, purchases.
- **Cashier**: Sales only.

## Language
Toggle between Arabic and English via the globe button at the bottom of the sidebar.
