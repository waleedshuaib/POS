# Sales & Checkout

The *POS* screen is built for fast work.

## Finding products
- Use the search field at the top to search by name or SKU.
- Scan a barcode — the scanner acts as a keyboard and adds the product automatically.
- Or click any product card in the left grid to add it to the cart.

## Editing the cart
- Increase/decrease quantity with the + / - buttons.
- Edit unit price or add a per-line discount in the cart row.
- Attach a customer via the dropdown.

## Checkout
- Click **Checkout** to open the payment modal.
- Add multiple payment rows (cash, card, credit…). Their sum must equal the total.
- Any overpayment is shown as change.

## Hold & resume
- Click **Hold** to save a cart under a name.
- From **Held Sales** you can resume any ticket.

## Returns
- From *Sales*, open a completed invoice and click **Return** to process a partial or full return.
