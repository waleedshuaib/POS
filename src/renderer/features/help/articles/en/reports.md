# Reports

From *Reports* you can view:
- **Sales by Day**: chart and CSV-exportable table.
- **Top Products**: best sellers in the period.
- **Profit & Loss**: revenue - cost of goods sold.
- **Payments by Method**: distribution across cash/card/…
- **Sales by Cashier**: to compare performance.
- **Inventory Valuation**: total stock value at cost.
- **Low Stock**: products at or below threshold.

Set the date range via the **From / To** fields and pick a tab. Use **Export CSV** to open the data in Excel or Google Sheets.
