# Daily Closing

Before starting the day: **Open the drawer** from *Drawer* and enter the opening amount.

During the day, every cash sale raises the *expected* amount; any change given is subtracted automatically.

At the end of the shift:
1. Count cash physically.
2. Enter the counted amount.
3. The system computes the **variance** and stores the closing record.

- Variance 0 = perfect match.
- Negative = cash shortage.
- Positive = overage (review receipts).

*Drawer* displays the history of past closings for accounting reference.
