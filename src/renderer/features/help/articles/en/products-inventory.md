# Products & Inventory

## Add a product
From *Products* click **Add product**. Fill in:
- **SKU**: unique per product.
- **Barcode**: optional, type or scan.
- **Arabic / English name**: appears on the receipt in the print language.
- **Price / Cost**: price for customer, cost for profit calculation.
- **Tax rate**: percentage (e.g. 16).
- **Track stock**: turn off for services or open items.
- **Low-stock threshold**: when on-hand qty reaches this, an alert shows.
- **Initial stock**: only for new products.

## Image
Pick an image; it is copied into the app's data folder so it works offline.

## Adjust inventory
From *Inventory*, click **Adjust** to add or subtract qty with a note. Every change is recorded in inventory movements.
