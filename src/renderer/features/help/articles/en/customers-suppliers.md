# Customers & Suppliers

## Customers
- Add regular customers to track purchases and credit balance.
- When paying with **Credit**, the amount is added to the customer balance.
- Use **Settle credit** to subtract a payment from balance.

## Suppliers
- Add suppliers and track what you owe them.
- From *Purchases*, record a purchase invoice: pick supplier, add line items with costs, and the amount paid.
- Any remaining balance is added to the supplier account.
- Inventory is updated automatically on save.
