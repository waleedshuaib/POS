# Printing & Hardware

## Thermal printer
The system supports ESC/POS thermal printers over USB or network.

To enable:
1. Open *Settings* → **Printer** section.
2. Enable **Thermal printer** and choose the type.
3. Click **Print** to test.

If you don't have a thermal printer, the app opens a PDF preview and uses your system printer.

## Arabic font
PDF receipts use the bundled Amiri font so Arabic renders correctly. No extra fonts needed.

## Barcode scanner
Any USB barcode scanner that emulates a keyboard (HID) works out of the box — no setup. Just make sure the focus is in the search input on the *POS* screen.
